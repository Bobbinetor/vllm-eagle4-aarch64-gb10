# Code of conduct

This project is a small, technical, unofficial build. The rules are short.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific and technical. "It does not work" is a starting point; a command,
  a log, and a version is a contribution.
- Disagree with the measurement, not the person. If a number looks wrong, say
  which method you used and what you got.
- Credit upstream: Eagle AI Corp. for EAGLE-4 and `eagle-harness`, the vLLM team
  for the engine, NVIDIA for the hardware.
- Respect the licenses. Do not ask for or share `eagle-harness` source,
  model weights, or anything that violates the EAGLE-4 license.
- Keep reports free of tokens, keys, and private paths.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Pressuring maintainers to remove the "unofficial" disclaimer or to claim
  endorsement by Eagle AI Corp. or NVIDIA.
- Spam, undisclosed self-promotion, or "first" claims without evidence.
- Publishing someone else's private information, logs, or credentials.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that break these
rules, and may block repeat offenders. If you see a problem, contact the
maintainer through the repository or a private security advisory.

This is a community build maintained on a best-effort basis. There is no SLA,
but reports are read and answered.
